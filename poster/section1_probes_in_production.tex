\section*{Probes in production}

\textbf{Activation probes are small classifiers that read a model's hidden states. In 2026 they moved from research into frontier serving, and every public deployment is a safety screen.}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Who} & \textbf{Model} & \textbf{How it is served} \\
\midrule
Anthropic       & Claude Opus 4.7--5.5   & Probe screens all traffic $\rightarrow$ classifier $\rightarrow$ block (cyber, prompt injection) \\
Google DeepMind & Gemini 2.5 Flash, 4 Argon & Mid-layer attention probe on input $\rightarrow$ Gemini classifier (cyber, bio) \\
OpenAI          & GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra     & During decode: pauses the stream $\rightarrow$ safety reasoner \\
Goodfire        & Kimi K3, GLM 5.3       & Probe in a \emph{private SGLang fork} $\rightarrow$ LLM judge (cyber) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cascade:} the probe screens 100\% of traffic and escalates ${\sim}$5--8\% to an LLM classifier.
  \item \textbf{The math is free:} an all-layer probe costs ${\sim}$377K FLOPs/token vs.\ ${\sim}$8 GFLOPs/token for a 4B model.
  \item \textbf{The serving is not:} no lab publishes latency or how activations leave its engine; every decode-time blocking probe runs on an engine its operator owns or forked.
\end{itemize}
